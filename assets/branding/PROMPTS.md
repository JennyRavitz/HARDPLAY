## Standalone symbol

It should convey two decks, crossfade, flow, precise live control. Strong coherent silhouette, sophisticated optical balance, chunky clean shapes readable at 24 pixels, like a world-class professional audio brand identity. Very simple 2–3 shapes total, not an equalizer, not headphones, not a generic music note, not an infinity symbol, not a chain link. One cool ice-blue ribbon and one near-white ribbon, subtle restrained blue depth, extremely crisp smooth edges, no fine lines. Centered symbol occupying about 60% of a square frame on a completely smooth dark navy #101C30 field. No app mockup, no extra objects, no text whatsoever, no neon, no green, no glow, no grunge, no noise, no drop shadow. Premium minimal emblem with a unique memorable silhouette.
